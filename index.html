I am a Postgraduate Associate at the Center for the Study of American Politics and the Tobin Center for Economic Policy at Yale University.

I graduated from Cornell University in 2024 with degrees in Information Science and Government.

My research interests include political behavior, political economy, democratic institutions, and quantitative methods. You can view my CV here.

Research summary:

My research focuses broadly on political behavior and political economy, particularly the ways political institutions, social identities, and economic conditions shape public opinion and political behavior.

I am especially interested in institutional trust, political polarization, democratic resilience, immigration and political incorporation, and the relationship between law and political behavior.

Methodologically, I am interested in causal inference, survey research, computational social science, and text analysis.

You can find additional information on my research page, GitHub, and LinkedIn.
