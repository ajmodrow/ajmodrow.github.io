I am a Postgraduate Associate at the Center for the Study of American Politics and the Tobin Center for Economic Policy at Yale University.

I graduated from Cornell University in 2024 with degrees in Information Science and Government.

My research interests include political behavior, political economy, democratic institutions, and quantitative methods. I am particularly interested in understanding how political institutions and economic conditions shape political attitudes and behavior.

Research

My current research focuses on public opinion, institutional trust, political polarization, and political behavior.

I am also interested in computational approaches to political science, including causal inference, survey research, and text analysis.
